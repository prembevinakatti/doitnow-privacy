Privacy Policy

DoItNow: Focus & Daily Tasks

Effective Date: November 7, 2026
Core Principle: DoItNow is engineered as a 100% offline, local-first productivity tool. We do not operate user accounts, we run zero cloud databases, and we never collect, track, share, or sell your personal data.

1. Data Collection & Transmission

DoItNow collects no personal data.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or name to use DoItNow.
  • Zero Remote Servers: We do not operate external web servers, analytics servers, or cloud backends. All task titles, descriptions, priorities, completion history, and streak metrics stay strictly on your device.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers: We do not integrate third-party tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Sentry, Facebook SDK, or advertising networks).

2. Local On-Device Storage

All application state is saved locally on your device using native on-device storage (AsyncStorage):

  • Task Data: Task titles, descriptions, priority levels, tags, creation dates, and completion status.
  • Streaks & Metrics: Aggregated daily completion counts used to render your productivity dashboard and streaks.
  • Preferences: User choices such as custom reminder times, quiet hours, and haptic intensity.

3. Android Permissions Explained

DoItNow requests only the minimal permissions required for on-device operation:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used solely to deliver local daily reminders and motivational nudges that you have configured. Notifications are scheduled and generated locally on your device without push tokens or remote servers.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used strictly to provide tactile haptic feedback when completing tasks or pressing buttons.
  • Run at Startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used by the Android operating system to restore your pending local notification alarms if you reboot your device.
  • Network Access (INTERNET): While Android frameworks provide network permissions by default, DoItNow does not establish any network connections. The external link to this Privacy Policy opens directly in your default browser.

4. Data Retention, Backup & Erasure

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain complete custody over it:

  • Manual Backup: You can export an unencrypted JSON backup of all your data directly within the app (Settings → Data Backup & Recovery).
  • Complete Deletion: You can permanently erase all tasks, history, and preferences at any time using the "NUKE ALL DATA" feature in Settings → Danger Zone, or by clearing app data in your device's Android System Settings.
  • Uninstalling: Deleting the application removes all locally stored data from your device unless you have enabled Android System Cloud Backups.

5. Children's Privacy

DoItNow does not collect or solicit information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application is a general-purpose offline productivity app.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices, we will post the revised policy at this URL with an updated effective date.
